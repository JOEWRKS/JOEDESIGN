# Independent Critique

Separate context, actual observations, claim evidence and calibration. Missing
execution/calibration is UNVERIFIED. Structural failures go in findings immediately.
Inspect original and result at corresponding whole-screen states. Separately assess
the dominant remaining visual gap, contract conformance, and actual motion sequences.
Static captures and functional endpoint tests cannot establish motion quality.
Do not replace these judgments with a blanket ship verdict or an invented score.
